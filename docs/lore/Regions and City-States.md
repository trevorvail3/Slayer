# Regions & City-States

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · powers: [[Factions]]

A loose geography of Aldermoor — the body of Aldmar ([[The Creation Myth]]). Keep it
flexible; add regions as zones are built. Names *(proposed)*.

## The shape of the land
- **The Bonereach** — the great mountain range, the Father's **bones/spine**. Cold,
  mineral-rich, and old; cyclopes and worse dwell in its deep places, and its bones
  sometimes *wake* ([[Bosses]]). Mining here is lucrative and cursed.
- **The Weeping Sea** — the western coast; the Father's blood and (say the Mourners)
  Vael's tears. Trade, piracy, drowned barrows.
- **The Olive Plains / the Reach** — dry grasslands between the poleis; where phalanxes
  meet and **centaur war-herds** run. Most Phase-1 fighting happens here.
- **The Hollow Wood** — wild forests (the Father's hair); satyrs, beasts, old shrines.
- **The Barrowlands** — fields of ancient graves where the **Ossuary Cult** works;
  the heart of the restless-dead threat.

## Zone-design intent
- The current combat zone reads as a stretch of **the Olive Plains / a contested pass**
  — human warbands, beasts, and cult-raised dead, with the **Bonereach** looming (home
  of the world boss / bone-Titan).
- Future biomes map cleanly to the regions above (coast, wood, barrowlands, mountains),
  each with a resident faction and monster set — a natural expansion path for the
  living-open-world system.
