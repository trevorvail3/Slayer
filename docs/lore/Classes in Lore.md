# Classes in Lore — The Three Orders

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · mechanics: [[Milestone Log]] (v0.6) · myth: [[The Creation Myth]]

A **Slayer** bears **Vael's Mark** — a mortal who fights like a demigod. That raw gift
is shaped by an **order**: a martial-sacred discipline you commit to at the **Shrine of
Paths**. Any Slayer may walk any path (Destiny model). Each order reads its power
through a different piece of the myth, which is what gives the classes *meaning*, not
just a kit.

## Warden — the Order of the Bulwark
*"The wall does not break. Neither does the Father."* Wardens embody **Aldmar** — the
bones, the mountains, the immovable. They are the shield-line made holy: heavy infantry
who hold when everyone else runs. Many come from **the Gift** or **the Bereaved** faiths
(endurance as devotion; the wall as the Father's steadfastness).
- Movement: **Shoulder Dash** — close the line. · Ability: **Barricade** — a wall of will
  and bronze. · Super: **Ground Slam** — the mountain answers.
- Fantasy lean: **Fortitude / Might**. Theme: *endure, anchor, protect.*

## Stalker — the Veiled
*"She struck once, from the dark, and made the world. So can you."* Stalkers embody
**Vael the First Slayer** — the unseen approach, the single killing blow. Hunters and
assassins who treat the night as an ally. Often drawn from **the Cunning** or from those
who quietly revere Vael herself.
- Movement: **Dodge Roll** — slip the blow. · Ability: **Caltrops** — deny the ground. ·
  Super: **Blade Storm** — one becomes many.
- Fantasy lean: **Swiftness / Ferocity**. Theme: *strike, vanish, finish.*

## Runecaster — the Ember-Marked
*"The old fire still answers those who dare ask."* The rarest order and the closest thing
Aldermoor has to true magic — which here is **sacred, not scholarly**. Runecasters are
god-touched battle-priests who carve **ember-runes** and call brief, awesome miracles:
fire from the sky, wards, and healing sigils. Their power is *limited and holy*, never
common wizardry (keeps the world grounded). Often of **the Mourning**, or of no faith but
the Mark itself.
- Movement: **Blink** — step the rune-path. · Ability: **Runic Rift** — a mending sigil. ·
  Super: **Meteor Storm** — the gods' fire called down.
- Fantasy lean: **Ferocity / Vigor**. Theme: *invoke, mend, unleash.*

## Why this matters
- Each order is a **stance on the myth**, so choosing a class is a small act of belief —
  fertile ground for dialogue, faction reactions ([[Factions]]), and questlines.
- The Runecaster is the *only* real magic in the world, and it is **rare and sacred** —
  in keeping with the grounded [[Tone and Naming]].
- Names are kept from the existing game; their *meaning* is what deepened. (Open to
  renaming an order if a better fit emerges.)
