# Classes in Lore

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · mechanics: [[Milestone Log]] (v0.6)

The three classes are **warrior-orders** — creeds a Slayer commits to at the
**Shrine of Paths**. Any Slayer can walk any path (Destiny model). Each pairs a
fighting philosophy with a signature power.

## Warden — Order of the Bulwark
**Creed:** *"The wall does not break."* The Bulwark held the passes when the muster
failed — heavy infantry who traded ground for nothing. Wardens fight as immovable
anchors: shield, barricade, and a shockwave that flattens what surrounds them.
- Movement: **Shoulder Dash** — close the gap, break a line.
- Ability: **Barricade** — raise a wall of will and iron.
- Super: **Ground Slam** — a detonation of raw might.
- Fantasy stat lean: **Fortitude / Might**.

## Stalker — The Veiled (Gloomstalkers)
**Creed:** *"Strike from the dark; leave nothing standing."* The Veiled learned to
move *through* the Blight's gloom rather than against it — scouts and assassins who
turned the enemy's murk into cover. Fast, evasive, lethal.
- Movement: **Dodge Roll** — slip through a blow untouched.
- Ability: **Caltrops** — seed the ground against the horde.
- Super: **Blade Storm** — a flurry that carves a pack apart.
- Fantasy stat lean: **Swiftness / Ferocity**.

## Runecaster — The Runewardens
**Creed:** *"The old marks still burn."* Runewardens kept the pre-fall craft of
ember-runes: wards, healing sigils, and called fire. The closest thing Aldermoor has
to mages, though their power is old and martial, not scholarly.
- Movement: **Blink** — step through space along a rune-path.
- Ability: **Runic Rift** — a sigil that mends those who stand in it.
- Super: **Meteor Storm** — call the sky down on the Blight.
- Fantasy stat lean: **Ferocity / Vigor**.

## Tone note
Keep the magic **martial-with-mystic** (see [[Tone and Naming]]): even the Runecaster
reads as a battle-priest of old fire, not a robed wizard. This is a world of iron
first, sorcery second.
