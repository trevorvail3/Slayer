# The Blight — The Enemy

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · see also [[Bosses]]

## What it is
**The Blight** is a corruption, not an army — a will-less spreading rot with a
terrible momentum. It seeps from **the deep wounds** (old rifts in the earth),
travels through root, water, and blood, and *remakes* the living into servants. It
does not command; it simply grows, and what it touches turns to its purpose.

Visually (and in the art direction): sickly veins of corruption, ashen skin, embered
cracks, twisted growth. See [[Tone and Naming]] for the palette.

## What it makes — the enemy roster
The game's three archetypes map to what the Blight does to different victims:

- **Husks** (in-game **Grunts**) — the Blighted dead of Aldermoor's fallen: soldiers,
  villagers, and travelers hollowed out and driven to attack the living. The bulk of
  the horde. Balanced, relentless.
- **Brutes** — victims the Blight *swelled* instead of hollowing: over-muscled,
  armor fused to flesh, slow but crushing. They barely feel a blow (knockback-resist).
- **Ravagers** (in-game **Archers**) — husks that kept a hunter's eye; they hang back
  and loose Blight-fouled shafts, herding you into the melee.

Later families (design hooks for [[Milestone Log]] expansion): **corrupted beasts**
(wolves, wyrms), **thornborn** (Blighted flora), and rarer **eldritch** things from
near the deep wounds.

## Its champions
Where the Blight concentrates, a **champion** forms — a focus of its will strong
enough to lead. These are the bosses:
- **The Warlord** — a fallen human general, mind half-intact, cruel and cunning.
- **The Colossus** — a mountain of corrupted earth, bone, and armor; a walking siege.
(Full detail: [[Bosses]].)

## Why Slayers matter
Slayers can't be hollowed, so they can do what armies couldn't: walk *into* the
Blight, break its champions, and cleanse the ground with **relics** and fire. Each
champion felled and each relic recovered pushes the rot back a little — the only
progress anyone has made in a generation.

## Open lore questions (to decide later)
- Is the Blight *sentient* at its core, or truly mindless? (Shapes the endgame.)
- What are the **deep wounds** — natural, or made? By whom?
- Can a Slayer be turned, given enough time? (A dark personal stakes thread.)
