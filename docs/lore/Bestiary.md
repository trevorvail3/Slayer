# Bestiary — The Enemy Roster

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · dead: [[The Dead and the Ossuary Cult]] · bosses: [[Bosses]]

Deliberately **grounded first, mythic later**. The three combat archetypes already in
game (melee / heavy / ranged) reskin cleanly across every tier below.

## Phase 1 — grounded (men, dead, beasts)
**Men** — the bulk of the wars:
- **Levies / raiders** — conscripts and brigands; the common melee soldier (grunt).
- **Warband champions / berserkers** — big men in heavy bronze, slow and crushing; the
  heavy (brute).
- **Skirmishers / peltasts / hunters** — javelin and bow from range (archer).
- **Phalanx spearmen** *(later flavor)* — disciplined shield-lines; a tactical enemy.

**The risen dead** — the Ossuary Cult's host ([[The Dead and the Ossuary Cult]]):
- **Draugr** (melee) · **skeletons/barrow-bones** (numbers) · **barrow-wights** (elite).

**Beasts** — the wilds:
- **Wolves / war-hounds** (fast packs) · **boars** (chargers) · **cave-lions** (ambush).

## Phase 2 — the mythic escalation
Rare, dreadful, and *earned* — the deep places and old ruins:
- **Centaurs** — war-herds of the Olive Plains; fast, ranged, disciplined cavalry-kin.
- **Minotaurs** — labyrinth-kin in old ruins; brutal, maze-bound elites/bosses.
- **Satyrs** — wood-dwellers; skirmishing tricksters.
- **Cyclopes** — giants of the Bonereach; slow, catastrophic, mini-boss scale.
- **Gorgons / harpies / hydra** — rarer horrors for strikes, raids, and set-pieces.
- **Bone-Titans** — the Father's bones woken to motion; the world-boss tier ([[Bosses]]).

## Design notes
- **Reskin, don't rebuild:** each tier is new meshes + stats + flavor over the existing
  archetype behaviors (chase/heavy/ranged, wind-up/stagger/knockback).
- **Escalation gates power fantasy:** killing men feels human; killing a cyclops or a
  waking Titan feels *mythic* — the roster is a difficulty and wonder curve, not just
  variety.
- **Tone:** even beasts and monsters are of **this world** (the Father's body) — no
  demons, no otherworldly corruption. See [[Tone and Naming]].
