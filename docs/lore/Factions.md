# Factions — The Powers of Aldermoor

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · faith basis: [[The Faiths and Schism]] · map: [[Regions and City-States]]

The political actors are **human city-states** plus one spreading heresy. Each wears a
faith, which gives it motive and enemies for free. **You earn reputation** with them
through quests, activities, and bounties — allying, opposing, or playing them against
each other. (Ties to the planned vendor-reputation / bounty systems.) Names *(proposed)*.

## The Bastion — your home *(unaligned)*
Once **Kaethon**; sacked and left a ruin; now the refuge you rebuild. Having lost its
patron faith when it fell, it belongs to no reading of the myth — which is exactly what
lets *you* choose how it leans. The neutral heart of your reputation web. Detail:
[[Factions and The Town]].

## Karnholt — the Ashen Shields *(warrior-supremacist)*
The Sparta of Aldermoor: an austere, honor-bound polis of phalanx purists who prize
strength and disdain softness. Faith: mostly **the Bereaved** (strength to one day
avenge the murdered Father). A fearsome rival and, when respected, a mighty ally.
*Activities:* honor-duels, war musters, holding the line at contested passes.

## Corvath — the Goldhand League *(cunning tyrant-city)*
Wealthy, clever, and treacherous; merchant-princes who revere **the Cunning** (Vael's
ruthlessness — the strong take). They deal in coin, secrets, and hired blades. Useful
and dangerous in equal measure. *Activities:* heists, escorts, blackmail, "problems
that need a Slayer and no witnesses."

## Sael — the Hallowed See *(priest-city)*
The seat of **the Gift** orthodoxy: temples, hierophants, and inquisitors who hold the
world is the Father's sacred body owed reverence. Generous to the pious, merciless to
heretics. *Activities:* pilgrim escorts, relic recovery, cleansing the restless dead,
hunting Deniers and cultists.

## Lyr — the Free Reckoners *(the Deniers)*
A city of philosophers, engineers, and skeptics who reject the gods entirely. The most
"modern," pragmatic power — and heretics to every faith above. *Activities:* recover
lost knowledge, protect their expeditions, debunk "miracles," rational bounties.

## The Ossuary Cult — the Palefathers *(antagonist heresy)*
Not a city but a spreading death-cult of necromancer-priests, an outgrowth of **the
Mourning** taken to a terrible conclusion: *if a god's death could be remade into a
world, then death is a door.* They raise the dead — the **draugr and skeletons** of
Phase 1 — from tombs, barrows, and battlefields. The region's primary early enemy.
See [[The Dead and the Ossuary Cult]].

## Design intent
- **Rep, not just vendors:** favor with each polis unlocks gear, quests, and story; some
  factions are mutually exclusive at the extremes (you can't be a Sael inquisitor *and*
  a Lyr heretic without consequences).
- **Nemesis hook:** the **[[Bosses|Warlord]]** is a *rival war-king* — a title, not one
  man — which lets procedurally-named tyrants rise from any faction as recurring foes.
- **Mythic peoples are NOT factions** — centaurs, minotaurs, cyclopes are monster
  threats and activities, not diplomacy ([[Bestiary]]).
