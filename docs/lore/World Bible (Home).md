# 📖 Slayer — World Bible

The canonical lore reference. **Canon v2**: a grounded **bronze-age / mythic** world
— think *300 in a Destiny game*. Iron, spears, phalanxes, feuding city-states, and a
world that is quite literally the body of a slain god. The supernatural is **rare and
sacred** in Phase 1 (men, the risen dead, and beasts); the mythic monsters
(centaurs, minotaurs, cyclopes) are the escalation.

> This is the single source of truth — keep the fiction consistent through it. Names
> marked *(proposed)* are placeholders open to change.

## The pitch in three lines
- The world is **Aldermoor** — the body of the slain World-Father **[[The Creation Myth|Aldmar]]**;
  his bones are the mountains, his blood the rivers.
- The Mother-goddess **Vael** slew him — the **First Slayer** — and *why* she did it is
  the great unanswered question that fractured mankind into warring faiths and
  **city-states** ([[The Faiths and Schism]], [[Factions]]).
- You are a **Slayer**, marked by Vael, a mortal who fights like a demigod — drawn to
  the ruined refuge of **the Bastion**, which you rebuild while you carve order back
  into a feuding, monster-haunted land.
