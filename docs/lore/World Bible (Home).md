# 📖 Slayer — World Bible

The canonical lore reference. Everything here is **draft v1** — a coherent
dark-fantasy foundation built to fit what's already in the game (the rebuildable
Bastion, the three warrior orders, the Warlord & The Colossus, relics, the Blight).
We revise as the story grows; keep this the single source of truth so the fiction
stays consistent.

## The pitch in three lines
- The realm of **Aldermoor** fell to **the Blight**, a corruption that hollows men
  into husks and swells beasts into monsters.
- You are a **Slayer** — rare of blood, unbroken by the Blight — drawn to the last
  free refuge, **the Bastion**, which you rebuild stone by stone.
- You gather, you rebuild, you arm, and you push back into the ruined wilds to break
  the Blight's champions and reclaim the light. (See [[Setting and Premise]].)
