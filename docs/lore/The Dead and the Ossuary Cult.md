# The Dead & the Ossuary Cult

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · faith root: [[The Faiths and Schism]] (the Mourning)

Phase 1 keeps the supernatural to a **single, disciplined thread**: the dead that will
not rest, and the human heretics who raise them. Everything else you fight early is
mortal — men and beasts ([[Bestiary]]).

## The Ossuary Cult (the Palefathers)
An outgrowth of **the Mourning** faith gone to a terrible conclusion. The Mourners
grieve a god's death; the Palefathers asked the forbidden question: *if Vael remade
Aldmar's death into a living world, then death is not an end — it is a door, and a door
can be opened.* They are **necromancer-priests**: pale-robed, bone-masked, patient.
They work the **Barrowlands** and old battlefields, and they see the risen dead not as
monsters but as *the saved* — proof that death can be mastered as a god was.

**What they want:** to prove death is conquerable, gather an army that cannot die, and
(the inner circle believes) one day *open the great door* — a heresy every faith would
burn the world to stop.

## The risen dead (Phase-1 enemies)
- **Draugr** — the recently, violently dead: fallen soldiers dragged back up in their
  own bronze, still fighting the last war. The bulk of the cult's host.
- **Skeletons / barrow-bones** — the long dead, stripped to bone; brittle but tireless,
  and unnerving in numbers.
- **Barrow-wights** *(elite)* — old chieftains and champions raised whole, cunning and
  strong; mini-boss material for tombs and cult sites.

They map cleanly onto existing enemy archetypes (a shambling melee **draugr** = grunt, a
heavy **wight** = brute, a bone-**archer** loosing from the dark). No new systems needed
to reskin — just flavor, later.

## Why it works for Phase 1
- **One** magical element, tightly sourced (human villains), keeps the world grounded
  while still giving you skeletons and dread.
- It's a **faction with motive** ([[Factions]]) — a campaign spine, not just a monster
  bin.
- It leaves the **truly mythic** (centaurs, minotaurs, waking bone-Titans) as a later,
  earned escalation ([[Bestiary]], [[Bosses]]).
