# The Creation Myth — The Slaying of Aldmar

← [[World Bible (Home)]] · leads to [[The Faiths and Schism]]

## The oldest story
Before the world, there were two: **Aldmar**, the Father, and **Vael**, the Mother.
What they were to each other — lovers, rivals, two halves of one thing — no story
agrees. What every story agrees on is the ending:

> **Vael slew Aldmar, and from his body she made the world.**

- His **bones** became the mountains — the **Bonereach**, the spine of the world.
- His **blood** became the rivers and the **Weeping Sea**.
- His **flesh** became the soil; his **hair**, the forests.
- His **last breath** became the winds and the sky.
- His **eyes** became the two great lights — the **Open Eye** (sun) and the **Closed
  Eye** (moon).

The land is called **Aldermoor** — *Aldmar's moor, Aldmar's realm* — because it is,
in the plainest sense the faithful can manage, **his corpse**. You do not walk *on*
the world. You walk on the Father.

## Where people came from
The common telling: the first people rose from the **clay where Aldmar's blood soaked
the earth** — children of the Father's body, quickened by Vael's act. But even this is
argued (see [[The Faiths and Schism]]): some say Vael shaped mankind from his marrow
on purpose; the **Deniers** say people simply *are*, and the gods are a story men told
to explain the mountains.

## The two unanswered questions
Everything downstream — every faith, every war — hangs on two things no one can prove:

1. **Why did Vael kill Aldmar?** Sacrifice? Betrayal? Grief? (This is *the* schism.)
2. **What became of Vael afterward?** Some say she departed the world she made; some
   say she sleeps within it; some say she still walks it unseen, taking the shape of a
   traveler, a crone, a killer — and that the **Slayers she marks** are her hand.

The game keeps both **deliberately unresolved** — it's the engine of the whole
setting's conflict and mystery. Answers can be doled out slowly (or never) through
the campaign.

## The Slayer's place in the myth
You bear **Vael's Mark**. Whether that makes you her chosen, her cursed, or merely a
mortal who can do the one thing she did — *slay what should not be slayable* — is your
story to earn. It is why you fight like a demigod in a world of mortals. See
[[Classes in Lore]].
